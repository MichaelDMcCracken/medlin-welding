Our Work

From trailer and dumpster repairs to full custom metal fabrication, we handle a wide range of welding projects. Backed by our SMAW certification, we bring proven skill and reliability to every job. With the flexibility to meet your specific needs, we deliver strong, precise, and lasting results you can count on. Whether it’s a small repair or a large-scale build, we approach each project with the same level of care and craftsmanship. Our goal is not only to meet expectations but to exceed them with quality work built to last.

One of my recent projects was to replace the bottom of this old Hardee Trailer. The before and after is like night and day. All he needs now is a cost of paint and it will look good as new! Give me a call to make your welding projects a reality!

Do you need repairs on your dog boxes this season? Or are you interested in having a custom dog box fabricated? Give us a call or send us a message!

A recent project completed was enhancing this farm truck to meet the customers towing needs. Do you have a project and need welding? Call us for an estimate!

This week mobile welding took me to Pittsboro to weld an excavator track pin that broke. Bee Legend Grading needed an emergency fix to finish their job. After a full days work doing county maintenance I headed over to Pittsboro and got them back up and running!

This pig cooker was sitting out in the rain for years and was never used. The bottom of the grill was completed rusted out and would flake off at single touch. We welded up 11 gauge sheet metal to the existing grill where the grill still had integrity. After cleaning the burners and putting the grill back together, it fired right up just like new!

About Us

Addison Medlin

Owner

Hi, my name is Addison Medlin, and I’m a state-certified welder. I started this business to provide reliable, high-quality welding services with a personal touch. I offer mobile welding and can come directly to your job site, or accept drop-offs at my location. Currently, I’m set up for stick welding, MIG welding, and stainless steel work. If you have a project in mind, give me a call—I’d be glad to help with your welding needs. Thank you for your support, and I look forward to sharing more completed projects with you!
